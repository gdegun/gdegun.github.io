Court Booking Assistant

Your court, booked the moment it opens.

Court Booking Assistant is a small private service that books tennis and padel courts, and group classes, for a handful of club members. Each member chooses their regular days, times and players. The assistant books those courts automatically the moment the club's booking window opens, then emails everyone where and when they're playing.

What it does

  • Books weekly courts at the exact moment they're released, nine days ahead.
  • Watches for cancellations on full courts and classes, and books a place when one frees up.
  • Sends confirmations and reminders by email, so every player knows the court, time and who's playing.
  • Keeps a simple account of each member's monthly subscription and payments, and emails statements and payment reminders.

How it uses Google

Court Booking Assistant sends its emails from its own Gmail account, courtbookingassistant@gmail.com, using the Gmail API with send-only permission. It can't read, search or delete any mail, and it doesn't access anyone else's Google account or data.

It's an independent service. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or run by any club or court operator.
