Court Booking Assistant

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Court Booking Assistant ("the service", "we") books racquet courts and classes for a small number of club members ("members"), and keeps track of their subscriptions. This policy explains what information the service holds, why, who it's shared with, and your choices. Contact: courtbookingassistant@gmail.com.

What we hold

  • Members' club login: the mobile number or email and password a member uses to sign in to their club's booking app. Used only to make, change or cancel that member's bookings.
  • Contact details: names, email addresses and phone numbers of members, and the names of the players they book with.
  • Bookings: dates, times, courts, classes and players for bookings the service makes or watches.
  • Subscription records: each member's monthly fee, charges, payments received and the reference they pay with. We don't hold card details. Payments are made by bank transfer, and we only record that a payment arrived.
  • Messages: a log of the emails and messages the service sends (recipient, subject, time), so a failed send can be noticed and retried.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to book courts and classes, to tell players about their bookings, to send schedules, statements and payment reminders, and to keep the subscription account right. We don't sell information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as below.

Google user data

The service uses the Gmail API with the gmail.send permission, on its own Gmail account only (courtbookingassistant@gmail.com), to send booking confirmations, reminders, schedules and statements. It doesn't read, store, search, delete or analyse any email, and it doesn't access any other person's Google account or data. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who it's shared with

  • The club's booking system: booking requests made on a member's behalf, using their login.
  • Google (Gmail): to deliver our emails.
  • DigitalOcean: the private server, in London, where the service runs and its data is kept.
  • Telegram: only for members who choose to link a Telegram chat, to send them the same notices.
  • Anthropic: the operator can ask an AI assistant questions about bookings and accounts, and the relevant booking details are sent to Anthropic's API to answer them. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.

Other players named on a booking see the booking details (court, time and players) in confirmation and reminder emails. Members never see each other's payment information.

How it's protected

Data is kept on a private server, reachable only over a private network and by key-based login, in files only the service can read. Any backups are encrypted. Logins are used only by the booking process and are never emailed or shown.

How long we keep it

While you're a member, and afterwards only as long as needed for the subscription records. When you leave, your login is deleted, your scheduled bookings stop, and you can ask for your other details to be deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, change your club password at any time, or stop using the service, by emailing courtbookingassistant@gmail.com. If you're in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and members will be told of any significant change by email.
